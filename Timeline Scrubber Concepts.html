Timeline scrubber — 5 concepts

Each concept targets one or more of the four complaints. All tracks are live — drag them.

  1. Ticks (שנתות) — A replaces event-notches with a calm decade ruler; C & E remove ticks entirely.
  2. Person indication — A uses a soft tinted range; B moves the life off the ruler onto its own lane.
  3. Marking things — C gives each kind of event its own glyph; E names the moment you land on.
  4. The handle — D compares four handles side by side; A/E try two of them in context.
Drag any track to scrub · concepts mix & match

A · Quiet ruler

Decade graduations instead of event notches · the year travels with the needle · selected person = soft tinted range

  • Ticks mark time (decades), not events — events become quiet dots on the baseline
  • The big serif year rides the needle as a flag, so eyes never leave the scrub position
  • Selected person: one warm translucent band with a name cap — no stage strips fighting the ruler
  • End labels (1903 / 2026) become the first and last tick labels — no separate right-edge block

B · Life lane

The ruler stays clean · the selected person gets their own thin lane below it, with birth/death caps and a label

1993scrubbing
  • Life stages keep their colours, but on a separate 5px lane — the scrub track is never overpainted
  • ● pink cap = birth, ○ hollow cap = passing; the label names the person and their span
  • That person's own events echo as faint stubs under the lane; everyone else's disappear
  • Deselecting a person simply collapses the lane — the ruler itself never changes

C · Typed markers

No ticks at all · each kind of event gets its own glyph below the line · selection dims everyone else's markers

1993scrubbing
birth marriage photo / moment passing — faded = not the selected person's
  • The ruler is a bare hairline; all meaning lives in the marker rail beneath it
  • With a person selected, their markers stay full-strength and everyone else's fade to 28%
  • Scrubbing near a marker names it inline (see the label under 1975) — no hover needed
  • Photos users have added get the blue square, so "where are the pictures?" is answered at a glance

D · Handle studies

Four handles at working scale — all draggable

Ring — current

White circle, blue border. Reads as a generic slider; easy to lose on a busy track.

Needle + flag

The year IS the handle. Precise hairline touch-point; frees the left readout entirely.

Drop pin

Points at the exact year from above; big friendly grab area, map-like "you are here".

Year lens

A dark pill carrying the year, sitting on the line. Boldest; handle and readout are one.

F · A + colors of B

A's quiet ruler and travelling year flag, but the person's band is tinted by their life stages instead of flat amber

  • New playhead handle: blue grab tab on top, hairline through the ruler, year pill riding below
  • Stage colours render at 45% opacity inside one rounded band, so they read as a wash, not strips
  • B's birth ● / passing ○ caps mark the ends; name cap stays above the band start

G · Default state — A + chapters

No person selected: same quiet ruler, but the generation chapters from E live on it as soft tinted blocks

  • This is the resting look; selecting a person swaps the chapter blocks for their stage band (F)
  • Chapter names sit above the ruler; the 20-year labels stay below, exactly as in A / F
  • Same playhead handle as F — grab tab above, blue year pill below — one component, two states

E · Chapters

No ticks, no notches — the track itself is banded into family eras, and the scrubber gently snaps to real moments

1993scrubbing
  • Alternating tinted bands = generations of the family; labels sit quietly underneath
  • Scrubbing within ~2 years of a real event snaps to it and names it in a small amber tag
  • Events are barely-there dots inside the bands — density is visible without any tick noise
  • The tall bar thumb matches the band height, so the "playhead" metaphor is unmistakable

H · Invite: beckon wiggle

A few seconds after entering, the tab sways twice — like a hand waving “grab me” — then rests (demo loops)

  • Nothing else on the bar moves — only the grab affordance calls attention to itself
  • In the app: plays once ~2s after load, again at ~10s if untouched, stops forever on first grab

I · Invite: peek back

On entry the playhead itself glides ~20 years into the past and returns — previewing exactly what scrubbing does (demo loops)

  • Strongest teach — the fill, dots and year all respond, so the mechanic explains itself
  • In the app: the tree would visibly time-travel with it; plays once on first load only

J · Invite: pulse + hint

A soft ripple breathes around the tab, chevrons hint the drag axis, and a one-time tooltip says it in words

  • Most explicit — good for first-run only; ripple + chevrons could stay as a quieter periodic nudge
  • All three dismiss on first interaction and respect prefers-reduced-motion
