AThe arrival
A warm pulse leaves the parents' ring and travels down the family line — the baby card blooms open where it lands, with soft ripple rings. Quiet, narrative: the child literally arrives from the line.
BBalloon lift
Three tiny pastel balloons carry the baby card up into its place. On touchdown the card gives a little bounce and the balloons let go, drifting up and away. Playful, storybook.
CBubble pop
The newborn floats up from between the parents inside a soap bubble, wobbling as it rises. At its spot the bubble pops into little droplets and sparkles and the card settles in. Bath-time cute.
DFamily sprout
A green shoot grows down the family line, unfurling a leaf or two on the way — and the card blossoms open at its tip inside a ring of soft petals. The family tree literally grows a new branch.
EPeek-a-boo
A little face pops out of the marriage ring, looks left and right — then hops down the tree in two bounces to its place, where the card catches it with a squash-and-stretch landing.
FShooting star
A star streaks down from the top of the sky trailing stardust, lands exactly where the child belongs, and bursts into twinkles as the card appears. A wish come true.
GCradle swing
The card swings in hanging from the parents' line on two strings, rocking like a cradle — each swing smaller — until it comes to rest and the strings quietly dissolve.
HHeartbeat
The marriage ring pulses twice, the family line draws itself downward in a lub-dub rhythm, and the card thumps in like a heartbeat — one small heart floats up and away.
IFireworks
A tiny rocket climbs from the bottom of the tree trailing sparks, bursts into a two-stage firework of palette-coloured streaks right above the child's spot — and the card pops in as the embers drift down past it.
JFireworks + pixel heart
Exactly the fireworks from I — rocket, triple burst, drifting embers — but when the smoke clears, a tiny 8-bit pixel heart is left hanging over the child for a moment before it floats away.
