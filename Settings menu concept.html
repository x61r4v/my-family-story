A settings menu for the top bar

Collapsing the language switch, Side-links and Family focus (plus room to grow) into one menu. Both options use the app's warm-paper style and keep each control's identity colour — plum for Side-links, amber for Family focus. Menus are shown open; grouped so new toggles drop into the right section.

Option A

Grouped popover

A gear button opens a roomy panel with labelled sections (like a document’s display settings). Segmented controls for either/or choices, toggles for on/off, a Reset in the footer. Scales best as the list grows.
