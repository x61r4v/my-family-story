The world on the timeline — 6 concepts

How do wars, COVID, the moon landing, and the night cable TV arrived in Israel live next to family birthdays? The mix is the point: serious and joyful, global and local, always tied back to who in the family was there.

  1. A · World rail — a quiet second shelf under the ruler; three glyph tones; hover for a whisper.
  2. B · Headline clippings — scrub past a moment and a newspaper clipping pops off the playhead.
  3. C1 · Ticker ribbon — a slim text band hugging the scrubber; the world drifts by at half speed, hover to read more.
  4. C2 · Horizon whispers — tiny stars always mark the horizon; scrub near one and it blooms into an icon + short title.
  5. D · Discover more — only landmarks visible; hover whispers, click opens the story; the rest waits in "+N more" pockets.
  6. E · The year speaks — no markers at all; the year readout itself narrates world + family.

B · Headline clippings

Nothing extra on the track — the news finds you: scrub past a world moment and a clipping pops off the playhead

1990scrubbing
  • The clipping's top edge carries the tone — grey for serious, amber for joyful, plum for pop culture
  • Every clipping ends with the family line in italic serif — the world event is always about us
  • Faint dots mark where clippings live, so the track hints without labeling
  • Clippings hold while you hover them (tap on touch) — a "read more" link opens the D-style story card

C1 · Ticker ribbon

A slim text band hugging the scrubber — no cards, just words and a tone glyph; the world drifts by at half speed

1996scrubbing
  • 64px tall and glued to the bar — it reads as part of the scrubber, not an invasion of the tree above
  • Plain text only; minors sit at 50% — you notice them when they drift past the playhead
  • Slide up from the handle onto any headline → a dark whisper opens with the story and the family line
  • Era names drift behind at half speed and the band re-tints per era — atmosphere without pixels on the track

C2 · Horizon whispers

Tiny stars always mark the horizon, inviting hover — scrub near one and it blooms into an icon + short title

1993scrubbing
  • Resting state is a constellation: tiny tone-colored stars and dots, loosely scattered on two rows — always there, hoverable, never loud
  • Scrub near a star and it blooms above the line: landmarks get icon + short title ("Internet", "Cable TV"); minors just the icon
  • Blooms parallax-drift toward their true position and fade by distance; the stars stay anchored
  • Hover a star or a bloom → the whisper opens with the full story + family line

D · Discover more

Progressive disclosure: only landmarks visible → hover whispers → click opens the full story with family ages · the rest hides in "+N more" pockets

1990explore
  • Three levels: glyph (always) → whisper tooltip (hover) → story card with the family-ages row (click)
  • "+3 more · the 90s" pockets keep density honest — click to spill the minor moments onto the rail
  • Story cards are where tone matters most: serious events get plain words, joyful ones get the anecdote
  • Later: a photo strip inside the card (gas-mask box, the modem, the Eurovision living room)

E · The year speaks

No markers anywhere — the year readout itself narrates: world context on one line, family context on the next

  • Cleanest track of all — the mix of serious/joyful lives purely in words and tone color
