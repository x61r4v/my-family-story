Adding to a moment — quieter affordances

Right now every expanded moment shows one big dashed “Add a photo” box. These directions make adding a photo, a location, or a written note feel lighter — and let you insert a whole new moment above or below an existing one, right from the life story. Same visual language as the app; hover/open states are shown frozen so you can read the interaction.
